Ukraine · Lead story

THE BRIDGE
FALLS

Ukraine’s second strike in two days drops the Kerch road arch into the strait — and cuts Russia’s road link to Crimea.

Ukrainian drones and missiles hit the 19‑km bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea overnight. Ukraine’s General Staff said an arch and a span of the road section collapsed; “the roadway is out of action: no vehicle can cross it.”

Crimea’s Russian-installed governor said two people were killed and traffic was suspended. Rail traffic resumed on one track. Russia hit Kyiv’s North and South bridges over the Dnipro in reply, disrupting heating, water and power.

Middle East

Tanker hit in the Strait of Hormuz

A tanker was struck by an unknown projectile on Tuesday about 7 nautical miles north of Khasab, Oman, setting its engine room on fire. Iran says tankers hit its mines. The USS Theodore Roosevelt has arrived — the third US carrier now in the region.

East Asia

Russian jets breach Japan’s skies

Two suspected Russian aircraft entered Japanese airspace off Okinoshima, Fukuoka, for about three minutes each from 4:07 a.m. Tuesday. Japan scrambled fighters and lodged an “extremely stern protest” — the first such breach since September 2024. South Korea scrambled jets too.

Europe

Five EU states plan a Rwanda return hub

Germany, Denmark, Austria, Greece and the Netherlands will negotiate a hub in Rwanda for rejected asylum seekers, announced in Vienna on Monday. Pilot: about 100 people; first transfers aimed for 2027.

United States

Pentagon fails its audit — again

The Pentagon failed its financial audit for the 9th straight year: auditors could not verify books covering $4.8 trillion in assets. 14 of 25 parts passed; a clean audit is promised by 2028.

Health

US loses measles-free status

The Pan American Health Organization said on Tuesday the US has lost the measles elimination status it held since 2000. Cases in 2026: 5,412, the most since 1991; 13 babies were born with measles.
